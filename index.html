Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 11, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Call Record Catch ("the App"), developed by Shahid Gul ("the Developer," "we," "us," or "our"), handles information when you use the application on your Android device. The App is designed to manage, play, and synchronize call-recording audio files that already exist on your device. The App does not itself record phone calls.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Please read it carefully.

1. Information and Files Accessed

Call Record Catch accesses only the information necessary to provide its core functionality:

  • Audio recording files that you select or make accessible to the App from a folder on your device.
  • File names and file metadata (such as file size, date modified, and file format) associated with those recordings.
  • Technical information required to process, display, and synchronize recordings (for example, upload status and queue position).

The App does not access your contacts, SMS messages, microphone, location, camera, or call logs. You choose which folder of existing recording files the App can access.

2. How Recordings Are Used

The recordings and associated file information you make available to the App are used for the following purposes:

  • Display: To list and present recordings within the App's interface.
  • Playback: To play recordings directly from within the App.
  • Synchronization: To synchronize eligible recordings with the configured backend server when synchronization is enabled.
  • Duplicate prevention: To track which recordings have already been uploaded and prevent duplicate uploads.
  • Upload status tracking: To maintain and display the upload status of each recording.

3. Uploading and Server Storage

When synchronization is enabled, eligible recordings may be uploaded from your device to the backend server configured by the Developer.

  • Uploaded recordings, along with associated file names and metadata, may be stored and processed on the backend server.
  • The purpose of uploading is to synchronize your recording files with the backend as configured within the App.
  • The App includes duplicate-upload protection and an upload queue to manage the synchronization process reliably.

We do not claim that recordings are end-to-end encrypted during transfer or storage. Data is transmitted over the network using standard protocols. See the Security section for more information.

4. Background Synchronization

Call Record Catch supports background synchronization, which allows the App to process and upload eligible recordings even when you are not actively using the App.

  • When background synchronization is enabled, the App may periodically check for new eligible recordings and upload them to the configured backend server automatically.
  • You can disable background synchronization at any time from the App's Settings.
  • When background synchronization is disabled, the App will not perform automatic background uploads.

5. Local Storage and Caching

The App may temporarily cache files on your device when required for playback or synchronization purposes.

  • Temporary cached files may be created during processing and are intended to be removed after the relevant operation is complete.
  • The original recording files on your device remain under your control and are not modified or deleted by the App.

6. Network Information

An active internet connection is required for the App to synchronize recordings with the backend server.

  • Without an internet connection, synchronization features will not function, but local playback and file browsing may still be available.
  • The App does not independently collect your IP address, device identifiers, or analytics data beyond what is strictly necessary for communication with the backend server during synchronization.

7. Third-Party Services

The App communicates with the Developer's backend API for the purpose of uploading and synchronizing recordings. This backend is operated or configured by the Developer.

The App does not integrate third-party analytics platforms, advertising SDKs, or other external service providers beyond the Developer's own backend infrastructure as described in this policy.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local data

Recording files stored on your device remain entirely under your control. You can add, remove, or manage these files at any time using your device's file manager or the App itself. Uninstalling the App does not delete your original recording files.

Server-side data

Recordings that have been uploaded to the backend server may remain stored on the server until they are deleted in accordance with the backend's data retention and deletion processes.

If you wish to request the deletion of your data from the server, please contact the Developer using the information provided in the Contact section below. We will process your request in a reasonable timeframe.

9. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data handled by the App and the backend server from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Call Record Catch is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the App, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

11. User Responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring that you use Call Record Catch only with recordings that you are legally authorized to access, store, process, and share.

Important: Laws regarding the recording of phone calls and conversations vary by jurisdiction. Many regions require the consent of one or all parties involved in a conversation before it may be recorded, stored, or shared. It is your sole responsibility to comply with all applicable recording-consent laws, privacy laws, and regulations in your jurisdiction before using this App to manage, play, or synchronize any recordings.

The Developer assumes no liability for any unauthorized or unlawful use of the App or the recordings processed through it.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality, legal requirements, or our practices. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

The updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how your information is handled.

Your continued use of the App after any changes to this Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
